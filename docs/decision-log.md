# Sales CRM decision log

These are decisions taken while building the application. The tech doc v2.1 (`01_Sales_CRM_Technical_Development_Document_Updated.md`) stays the reference. This log records where the build follows a recommended default, or departs from the doc, and why.

**Labels:**

- **[BO]**: decided by the business owner (Rajkiran).
- **[DEF]**: the doc's recommended default, applied because the open decision (OD) is still open.
- **[IMPL]**: an implementation choice made by the developer. It changes no business rule.
